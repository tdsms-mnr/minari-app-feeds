Jewelday

日本語

Keep every piece of jewelry you own in one place, choose what to wear today, and rediscover the pieces you forgot you had.

What you can do

  • Photograph a piece and the background is removed on your iPhone. Lay several out and one photo adds them all.
  • Choose today's pieces for your ears, neck, wrist and fingers. Save the combinations you wear often as sets.
  • See pieces you haven't worn in a while, and how often you wore each one this year.
  • Keep metal, stones, where it came from, price, care dates, and certificates with each piece.

Free and Pro

Free

  • Up to 5 pieces

  • Today, rediscover, wearing records

Jewelday Pro

  • As many pieces and sets as you like
  • Certificate photos and PDFs

Questions

Where is my data stored?Only on your iPhone. There is no account, and your photos and records are never uploaded. See the Privacy Policy.
I bought Pro on another iPhone.Open Settings (gear) and tap Restore Purchases.
Some pieces weren't separated from the photo.Small pieces placed close together can be missed. Tap "Trace to add" and draw around them. Use the eraser to clean up the inside of rings.

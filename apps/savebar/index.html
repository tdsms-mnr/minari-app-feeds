Questions people ask

Is there a savings goal tracker without a subscription?Yes. Savebar's Small widget and one goal are free, and Pro — more goals and bigger widgets — is a one-time purchase.
How many goals can I make for free?One. Savebar Pro (one-time purchase, no subscription) gives you unlimited goals.
Can I see my progress on the Lock Screen?Yes. Lock Screen widgets (a ring with your percentage, what's left to go, and a one-line view) are part of Savebar Pro. The free version has the Small widget on the Home Screen.
Does it connect to my bank?No. You add amounts yourself, so there are no logins and nothing to link.
Can I add money without opening the app?Yes. Tap the amount button (like +$10) on the Home Screen widget. To add a different amount, open the app and tap “+ Add”. Step-by-step guide
Where are my goals and amounts stored?On your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded.
How do I change the amount on the widget button?Open the goal, tap “…” › Edit, and change “Widget amounts”. Medium and Large widgets can show a second amount.
How do I delete a goal?Touch and hold the goal in the list and tap Delete. You can undo it for a few seconds.
A store link didn't fill in the photo.Some stores don't let apps read their pages. Choose a photo from your library instead.

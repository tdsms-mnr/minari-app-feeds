Photo widget cutting off faces? How to fix it on iPhone

A widget is a fixed shape. Your photo usually isn't. When the two don't match, the widget has to crop something — and with portraits, that is often the top of someone's head or the person standing at the edge.

In Mantel there are two ways to fix it. Both are free.

Why faces get cut off

A Small widget is square. Most iPhone photos are 3:4 tall or 4:3 wide. To fill the square, part of the photo has to go. By default the crop is taken from the middle of the photo, so a face near the top or side can fall outside it.

Fix 1: choose the spot to keep in view

Best when you want the widget filled edge to edge, but centered on a face.

  1. Open Mantel and open the set that has the photo.
  2. Touch and hold the photo, then tap “Adjust position”.
  3. Tap or drag the spot you want to keep in view. The Small and Medium previews below the photo change as you move it.
  4. Tap “Save”. The widget now crops around that spot.

You can set a different spot for each photo. To go back to the middle, tap “Center”.

Fix 2: show the whole photo

Best when nothing in the photo should be cut — a group shot, or a photo with text in it.

  1. Open the set.
  2. Next to “Photo”, choose “Whole photo”.

The widget shows the entire photo without cropping, with a small margin around it. This applies to every photo in the set. “Fill frame” switches back to filling the widget.

Which one should I use?

  • One person, or a pet — Fix 1. The widget stays full and the face stays in.
  • Several people, or a wide landscape — Fix 2. Nobody gets cut.
  • A mix — keep separate sets, for example one set on “Fill frame” and one on “Whole photo”, each in its own widget (more than one set needs Pro).

Questions

Does adjusting the position change my original photo?No. Mantel only remembers which spot to keep in view. Your original photos are never changed.
Is this a Pro feature?No. “Adjust position” and “Whole photo” are both free. The free version includes one Small widget; Medium and Large widgets are Pro.
How do I undo it?Open “Adjust position” again and tap “Center”, then “Save”.

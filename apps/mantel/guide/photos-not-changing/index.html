Photo widget not changing? What to check on iPhone

You picked several photos, but the widget keeps showing the same one. In most cases it is one of four settings.

Here is what to check in Mantel, a photo widget app for iPhone.

1. The set has only one photo

The widget moves to the next photo in the set. A set with one photo has nothing to change to. Open the set and tap Add to add two or more.

2. The change interval is longer than you think

In the set, “Change every” decides how often the photo changes.

  • Free: every hour
  • Pro: every 15 minutes, every hour, every 6 hours or once a day

If it is set to “Once a day”, the photo changes only once a day.

3. The widget shows a different set

Each widget shows one set. Touch and hold the widget, tap Edit Widget, and check the Set. If none is chosen, it shows your first set.

4. A Medium or Large widget shows a lock

Medium and Large widgets are part of Mantel Pro. In the free version, use the Small widget.

Questions

How often does the photo change?Every hour in the free version. With Mantel Pro, choose every 15 minutes, every hour, every 6 hours or once a day in “Change every”.
Can the photos come in random order?Yes, with Pro: turn on “Shuffle” in the set.
I added photos but the widget still shows the old ones.Check that the widget shows the set you added them to: touch and hold the widget, tap Edit Widget, and choose the Set.
It still doesn't change.In Mantel, go to Settings → “Contact / report a problem” to send an email. The app version, iOS version and iPhone model are filled in for you.

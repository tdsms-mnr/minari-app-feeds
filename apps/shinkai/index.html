Keep animals from real deep-sea places. Shine a light to see behaviors that have really been observed, and new animals arrive as the days pass.

What's free

Free

  • One starter sea of three: Monterey Canyon, East Pacific Rise or Gulf of Mexico
  • Its animals, behaviors and visitors
  • The journal and observation cards

More seas

  • Each other sea is a $3.99 one-time purchase. No subscription.
  • Any sea you add also unlocks the Home Screen widget.

Questions

Where is my sea stored?Only on your iPhone. There is no account. See the Privacy Policy.
I bought a sea on another iPhone.Open Settings (gear), then Purchases, and tap Restore Purchases.
I can't hear the sea.The sound follows your iPhone's silent switch. Turn silent mode off, or check Sound in Settings (gear).
Do the animals go hungry if I don't open the app?No. Nothing dies, and nothing is lost while you are away.

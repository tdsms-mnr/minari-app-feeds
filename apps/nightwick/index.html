Nightwick

日本語

Keep the candles you have on one shelf, choose one for tonight, light it, and keep the ones you burn to the end in an album.

What you can do

  • See every candle you have, with its photo, name and scent. Tap one and it becomes tonight's candle.
  • Paste a shop page link and the photo, name, scent notes and weight are filled in.
  • On a candle's first night, see a time to let it burn until, so the wax can melt across the top.
  • Candles you burn to the end move to the album, with every night you lit them.

Free and Pro

Free

  • Up to 3 candles on your shelf
  • Lighting and the first-night time
  • The album and your scent families

Nightwick Pro

  • As many candles as you like
  • The Lock Screen while a candle burns
  • Home Screen widget
  • iCloud, so your candles stay when you change iPhones
  • A scent family to try next
  • One-time purchase. No subscription, no ads.

Questions

Where is my data stored?On your iPhone. With Pro, also in your own iCloud. There is no account. See the Privacy Policy.
I bought Pro on another iPhone.Open Settings (gear) and tap Restore Purchase.
A shop link didn't fill everything in.Some shops don't show these details on the page. Type in what's missing yourself.

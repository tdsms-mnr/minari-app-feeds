Questions

How do I set a print as my wallpaper?Open a print and tap Save as wallpaper. The wallpaper goes to your photo library. Then, on your Lock Screen, touch and hold, tap +, and choose Photos.
"Full height" takes a moment the first time.Full-height images are larger, so they are downloaded from Apple the first time you use a series. After that they work offline.
How do I add the widget?Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose Ukiyobook. The Small widget is free; Medium and Large come with Pro.
I bought Pro on another iPhone.Open the Albums tab, tap any locked album, then Restore purchase.
Where are my albums stored?Only on your iPhone. There is no account. See the Privacy Policy.
Where do the images come from?All prints are in the public domain. Images from the Art Institute of Chicago, The Metropolitan Museum of Art, the Brooklyn Museum and the National Diet Library of Japan.

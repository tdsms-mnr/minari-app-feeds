Farwalk

From your home to someone else’s — the real distance, walked.

How to use & FAQ · 使い方とよくある質問 · Preguntas frecuentes

Pick any two places on Earth. Farwalk measures the real straight-line distance between them and moves you along it with the walking distance Apple Health already records. Your Lock Screen shows how far is left. Walk one journey with someone from both ends, and meet in the middle.

How it is paid for

  • Everything is a one-time purchase. No subscription.
  • No ads. No account. Sharing uses your iCloud, not a developer server.
  • Free walks one journey at a time, any distance, and keeps every finished journey.
  • One more journey $2.99 (buy as many as you like) · Pro $9.99 (three journeys at once, Expeditions & Film Locations, gold badges, arrival forecast, your own theme colors) · Pro for two $14.99.

What it does

  • Any start and any destination: an address, a city, a friend’s house
  • Destinations to collect: world landmarks, natural wonders, great cities, theme parks and dream beaches — grouped by how many days they are at your pace. Pro adds Expeditions (the Moon, the Poles, around the world) and Film Locations
  • Uses your Health walking + running distance directly — no step-to-distance guessing
  • Lock Screen and Home Screen widgets
  • Walk together: share a journey and meet in the middle, walk side by side, or add up as a team
  • Every finished journey becomes a badge — kept free or Pro

Why is the widget a little behind?

iOS refreshes third-party widgets on a budget — roughly every 20 minutes during the day, and only a few dozen times a day in total — and Health data can only be read while your iPhone is unlocked. So the widget can lag your latest steps by up to about 20 minutes. Opening Farwalk, or tapping the widget, catches it up right away. This is how iOS treats every walking app; it is not a setting in Farwalk.

Requirements

  • iPhone with iOS 17 or later
  • Apple Health with walking distance (an Apple Watch improves accuracy but is not required)
  • Sharing a journey needs iCloud on both iPhones

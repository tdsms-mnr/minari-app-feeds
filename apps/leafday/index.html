Leafday

Which plants need water today? Tap the drop. No subscription.

日本語

Leafday shows which of your plants need water today, right on your Home Screen. Tap “Water” on the widget and it's done. No plant identification, no AI diagnosis, no subscription — just your pots and how many days until each is thirsty.

Pricing, plainly

  • Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.
  • No ads. No account.
  • The Small widget stays free forever.

What it does

  • Today's plant and a water button on the free Small widget
  • As many plants as you like, with optional photos
  • Suggested intervals for common houseplants — always a guide: check the soil first
  • Pro: Medium and Large widgets, Lock Screen widgets, fertilizing, misting and repotting schedules, rooms with a widget per room, travel mode, a morning reminder, weather-aware watering, iCloud sync, and a care review
  • Available in 18 languages
  • iOS 17 or later

How to use

  1. Tap “+” at the top right to add a plant. A name and a watering interval are all it needs; photos and rooms can come later.
  2. After watering, tap the drop in the list, or “Water” on the widget. To undo, tap the drop in the list again.
  3. To add the widget, touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen → Edit → Add Widget → Leafday.
  4. Weather adjustments (Pro): turn on “Weather adjustments” in Settings. For plants outside, also turn on “Outdoors” in the plant’s edit screen. They wait a day after rain, and watering comes a day sooner on hot days.
  5. Travel mode (Pro): in Settings, “Plan a trip” with the day you leave and the day you’re back. Schedules pause while you’re away and move back by the days you were gone.
  6. iCloud sync (Pro): turn on “Sync with iCloud” in Settings on both iPhones. Photos are not synced.
  7. After reinstalling or switching iPhones, tap “Restore Purchases” in Settings to get Pro back.

Questions people ask

Is there a plant watering reminder without a subscription?
Yes. Leafday's Small widget and unlimited plants are free, and Pro is a one-time purchase.

Can I mark a plant as watered without opening the app?
Yes. Tap “Water” on the Home Screen widget. Step-by-step guide

Does it identify plants?
No. Leafday deliberately skips identification so it can work without a server or a subscription.

Where are my plants and photos stored?
On your iPhone. Only if you turn on iCloud sync, everything except photos is matched to your other iPhone through your own iCloud. Nothing reaches the developer.

Where does the weather come from?
Apple Weather (WeatherKit). Your location is rounded to about 11 km and used only to look up the weather.

It rained, but my schedule didn't move.
Weather adjustments only move plants with “Outdoors” turned on. Indoor plants only come a day sooner on very hot days.

Where can I send a bug report or a request?
From “Contact / report a problem” in the app’s Settings. Every message is read.

Privacy

Your plants and photos stay on your device. Only if you turn on iCloud sync, everything except photos is matched through your own iCloud. Privacy policy.

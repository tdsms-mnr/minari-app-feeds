How to see which plants need water today on your iPhone Home Screen

Watering is easy to forget when every plant is on a different schedule. A widget shows the plant that needs water today where you already look, and you can mark it watered with one tap.

Here is how to set it up with Leafday. The Small widget and as many plants as you like are free.

1. Add your plants

  1. Open Leafday and tap + at the top right.
  2. Enter a name, for example “Monstera”.
  3. Under How often, set the number of days. Common plants (rough guide) suggests an interval for common houseplants.
  4. Optional: set when you last watered it under Last watered, and add a photo.
  5. Tap Save. Add as many plants as you like.

The suggested intervals are only a guide — pots, light and season change a lot. Check the soil first: water when the top inch is dry.

2. Put the widget on your Home Screen

  1. Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen until the apps jiggle.
  2. Tap Edit (or +) in the top corner, then search for “Leafday”.
  3. Pick the Small size and tap Add Widget.

The Medium and Large sizes are part of Leafday Pro. In the free version they show a lock.

3. Tap Water when you water

The widget shows a plant that needs water today, how many are left, and a Water button. After watering, tap Water. The next plant comes up without opening the app. When everything is done, it says All watered and which plant is next.

In the app, you can also tap the drop next to each plant. Tap it again to undo.

Petalbook

日本語

Turn a flower you were given, or one you picked on a walk, into a pressed specimen. Photograph it, close your phone, and wait a few days.

Free and Pro

Free

  • Up to 3 flowers
  • Labels with the name, who gave it and the place
  • Save a specimen as an image

Petalbook Pro

  • As many flowers as you like
  • $5.99, one-time purchase. No subscription.

Questions

Where are my photos stored?Only on your iPhone. There is no account, and your photos are never uploaded. See the Privacy Policy.
I bought Pro on another iPhone.Open Settings (gear), tap Petalbook Pro, then Restore Purchase.
Does the Example specimen count toward the 3 flowers?No. Only your own flowers count. Delete one and you can press another.
Does it work on iPhones that don't fold?Yes. Tap Press instead of folding the phone.

Awayglass

日本語

An hourglass that runs only while your phone is closed. On iPhones that don't fold, lay it face down.

Free and Pro

Free

  • The hourglass and today's time
  • History and CSV export
  • A 3-hour glass
  • The Lock Screen timer for your first glass

Awayglass Pro

  • The Lock Screen timer, every time
  • A glass from 1 to 12 hours
  • $5.99, one-time purchase. No subscription.

Questions

Where are my records stored?Only on your iPhone. There is no account. See the Privacy Policy.
I bought Pro on another iPhone.Open Settings (gear), tap Awayglass Pro, then Restore Purchase.
The Lock Screen timer doesn't appear.Check that Live Activities are on for Awayglass in the iOS Settings app.
Does it work on iPhones that don't fold?Yes. Lay the phone face down to start, and pick it up to stop.

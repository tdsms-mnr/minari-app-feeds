Driftfall

日本語

Let snow, sakura petals, maple leaves and more fall over your own photos. Set it as your Lock Screen, or send it as a moving photo.

What you can do

  • Choose a photo and pick what falls on it. Adjust how much falls and how the wind blows.
  • Save it as a Live Photo and set it as your Lock Screen. It starts to fall for a moment when you wake your iPhone.
  • Send it as a short video that loops without a seam.
  • 14 to choose from: sakura, daisies, hydrangea, rain, fireflies, maple leaves, ginkgo, fallen leaves, snow, feathers, bubbles, confetti, stardust and shooting stars.

Free and Pro

Free

  • Try all 14 on your own photos
  • Save and send rain and bubbles

Driftfall Pro

  • Save and send all 14
  • One-time purchase. No subscription, no ads.

Questions

How do I set it as my wallpaper?After saving, open Settings > Wallpaper > Add New Wallpaper > Photos, and choose the Live Photo you saved.
It only moves for a moment.That is how iOS plays a Live Photo wallpaper: briefly, when you wake your iPhone. To watch it longer, send it as a video.
Where are my photos stored?Only on your iPhone. There is no account and nothing is uploaded. See the Privacy Policy.
I bought Pro on another iPhone.Open Settings (gear) and tap Restore Purchase.

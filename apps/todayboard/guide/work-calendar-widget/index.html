How to show only your work calendar in an iPhone widget

If your iPhone holds both work and personal calendars, a widget that shows everything gets crowded. A widget with just your work calendar shows what's next at work, and nothing else.

Here is how to do it with Todayboard, a calendar widget that reads the calendars already on your iPhone. Choosing calendars is part of Todayboard Pro, a one-time purchase. The free Small widget shows all your calendars.

1. Check that your work calendar is on your iPhone

Todayboard reads the events in your iPhone's Calendar app. If your work calendar isn't there yet:

  1. Open Settings → Apps → Calendar → Calendar Accounts (on iOS 17: Settings → Calendar → Accounts).
  2. Tap Add Account and add your work account — for example your Google account.
  3. Turn on Calendars for that account.

2. Allow calendar access

Open Todayboard, tap Continue and allow full access. Access is read-only: Todayboard never edits your events.

Declined by mistake? iPhone won't ask again. Open Settings → Calendars and choose Full Access.

3. Choose your work calendars (Pro)

  1. On Todayboard's main screen, find the DISPLAY card.
  2. Tap “Show calendars” and check only your work calendars.
  3. Optional: turn on “Hide all-day events” to hide birthdays and holidays. This one is free.

4. Put the widget on your Home Screen

  1. Go to your Home Screen. Touch and hold an empty area until the apps jiggle.
  2. Tap Edit in the top-left corner, then Add Widget.
  3. Search for “Todayboard”, pick a size and tap Add Widget.

5. Work and home on separate widgets (Pro)

Each widget can show its own calendars. Touch and hold a widget, tap Edit Widget, then choose its Calendars. A widget with nothing chosen follows “Show calendars” in the app.

Questions

Does it show my Google Calendar?Yes. Add your Google account in Settings → Apps → Calendar → Calendar Accounts → Add Account and turn on Calendars. Its events then appear in the iPhone Calendar app, and Todayboard shows them. Todayboard never signs in to Google itself.
Can I do this for free?The free Small widget shows today's events from all your calendars, and “Hide all-day events” is free too. Choosing which calendars show is part of Todayboard Pro, a one-time purchase, along with Medium and Large widgets, Lock Screen widgets and tomorrow or the next 7 days.
My work calendar isn't in the list.Todayboard reads the calendars set up on your iPhone. Add the account in Settings → Apps → Calendar → Calendar Accounts → Add Account (on iOS 17: Settings → Calendar → Accounts), turn on Calendars for it, then come back to Todayboard.
The widget says “Choosing calendars is Pro.”Calendars were chosen in Edit Widget without Pro. Remove them in Edit Widget to show all calendars again, or get Todayboard Pro.
Does Todayboard change or upload my events?No. Calendar access is read-only, and nothing leaves your device.

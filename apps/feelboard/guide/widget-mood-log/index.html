How to log your mood from an iPhone widget without opening the app

A mood log works best when it takes one second. With a widget on your Home Screen, you tap how you feel right there — no app to open, nothing to type.

Here is how to set it up with Feelboard. The Small widget is free.

1. Put the widget on your Home Screen

  1. Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen until the apps jiggle.
  2. Tap Edit (or +) in the top corner, then search for “Feelboard”.
  3. Pick the Small size and tap Add Widget.

2. Tap a mood

The widget asks “How are you?” and shows five colored circles: Great, Good, Okay, Low and Awful. Tap one. It is logged, and the widget shows today's mood.

  • Tapped the wrong one? Tap the right one within 10 minutes and it replaces the last one.
  • You can log again later in the day. Each day becomes one colored square, in the color of the last mood you logged that day.

3. Add a note (optional)

After you log, the widget shows “Add a note”. Tap it, and Feelboard opens with “Add a note (optional)” for one line about your day.

Feelboard is a simple personal log, not a medical or mental-health service.

Questions

Is the widget free?Yes. The Small widget is free, and you can log as often as you like. No ads, no account.
Can I change the five moods?With Feelboard Pro, a one-time purchase: in Settings, tap “Edit moods” to rename them, change colors or add your own. The first five appear on the widget.
Can I see my whole year?Yes, with Feelboard Pro: “Your year in pixels” in the app, and a Large widget with this month's calendar and your year as one strip.
Where are my moods stored?On your iPhone. There is no account and nothing is sent to the developer. If you turn on iCloud sync (Pro), a copy is kept only in your own iCloud.

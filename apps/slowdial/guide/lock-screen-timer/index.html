How to show a visual timer on your iPhone Lock Screen

A disc that shrinks as time passes shows “how much is left” without reading a clock. If it stays on the Lock Screen, you can lock your iPhone and still see it.

Here is how to do it with Slowdial, a visual timer with no ads. This part is free.

1. Pick a time

  1. Open Slowdial and tap a preset, from 1 minute to 1 hour.
  2. Need a different time? Tap “Custom” and set it.
  3. Optional: under the disc, choose “Whole circle” (the time you pick fills the whole circle) or “60-min dial” (like a classic 60-minute dial).

2. Check “Show on Lock Screen”

On the main screen, “Show on Lock Screen” should be on. It is on by default.

3. Start and lock

  1. Tap “Start”.
  2. Lock your iPhone. The disc keeps shrinking on the Lock Screen, and in the Dynamic Island on iPhones that have one.
  3. When time is up, the end sound plays, even with the screen locked.

If the disc doesn't show

  • Open the iPhone Settings app, tap Slowdial and check that Live Activities is on.
  • Timers longer than 8 hours can't be shown on the Lock Screen (an iOS limit). The end sound still plays.
  • If Slowdial shows “Notifications are off”, tap it and allow notifications. Otherwise the end sound won't play while the screen is locked.

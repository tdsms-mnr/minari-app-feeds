How to check off to-dos from your iPhone Home Screen widget

A to-do list you have to open is a list you forget. With a widget, today's things sit on your Home Screen, and you check them off right there.

Here is how to set it up with Threeline, which keeps it to three things a day. The Small widget is free.

1. Write today's three

  1. Open Threeline. It asks “What are today's three?”
  2. Write one thing each in “Thing 1”, “Thing 2” and “Thing 3”.

2. Put the widget on your Home Screen

  1. Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen until the apps jiggle.
  2. Tap Edit (or +) in the top corner, then search for “Threeline”.
  3. Choose the Threeline widget. (Note is a different widget, for one sticky note.)
  4. Pick the Small size and tap Add Widget.

3. Tap to check off

When you finish something, tap the circle next to it on the widget. It is checked off right there, without opening the app. Tapped by mistake? Tap it again to undo.

4. A new day starts empty

Each day starts with three empty lines. If something from yesterday wasn't done, open Threeline and tap “Carry over” under “Left from yesterday”. Anything you don't carry over stays in yesterday.

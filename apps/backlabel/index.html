Backlabel

Photograph the ingredient list on a Japanese or Korean product, and Backlabel shows every ingredient in English, in the order the label lists them, with what each one does. No scores, no ratings.

What's free

Free

  • Read as many labels as you like
  • My list and notes
  • Keep up to 10 labels

Pro (one-time purchase)

  • Keep every label
  • Compare two labels side by side
  • Search your saved labels by ingredient
  • No subscription

Questions

Where are my labels stored?Only on your iPhone. There is no account. See the Privacy Policy.
An ingredient says "Not sure yet".Backlabel does not guess. Tap it, then pick one of the possible matches, or tap Type and enter the name.
The directions are not translated.Translation runs on your iPhone (iOS 18 or later). The first time, iOS may need to download the language. Connect to the internet and open the label again.
I bought Pro on another iPhone.Tap the gear on the camera screen, then tap Restore purchase.
Is this medical advice?No. Backlabel explains what is written on a label. If you have an allergy or a skin condition, ask a doctor or pharmacist.

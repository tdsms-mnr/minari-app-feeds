Driftfall Privacy Policy

日本語

If the English and Japanese versions differ, the Japanese text is authoritative.

Your photos

To start, you choose a photo with the iOS photo picker. Driftfall ("the app") receives only the photo you choose; it does not access your whole photo library. The app keeps a copy of that photo and your settings (what falls, how much, the wind) only on your device, in the app's own container, so you can come back to it. The app does not send your photos to the developer's servers and does not share them with third parties. There is no account and no advertising.

Saving to Photos

When you save a Live Photo or a photo, the app asks for permission to add to your photo library. It only adds the picture you save; it does not read your library.

Sending a video

When you tap "Share as Video", the video is made on your device and handed to the iOS share sheet. It goes only where you choose to send it, such as Messages. It never passes through the developer's servers.

Anonymous usage

To improve the app, the following anonymous records are sent to the developer's server.

  • The kind of action taken, such as opening the app, choosing a photo, saving or sending
  • Which of the 14 things you chose to let fall
  • The number of photos you have made in the app (never the photos themselves)
  • Which screen showed the Pro offer, and the result of a purchase (completed, cancelled, failed)
  • App version, OS version and language setting
  • A random identifier generated on the device (deleted when you delete the app)
  • The country of the connection (IP addresses are not stored)

The following are never sent:

  • Your photos, or anything in them
  • Contact details such as name, email address, phone number or postal address
  • Advertising identifier (IDFA) or device identifier (IDFV)
  • Location
  • IP address

These records are not used to serve or measure advertising, are not sold or given to data brokers, and are not linked with any third party's tracking data. No third-party analytics SDK is included.

You can stop sending at any time from Settings (gear) > Privacy > "Send anonymous usage data".

Bookspine Privacy Policy

日本語

Your information

Bookspine stores the books you add — title, author, page count, reading progress and the date you finished — only on your device, in the app's own container. This information is never sent to the developer's server and is never shared with third parties. No account is required and no ads are shown.

Only if you turn on iCloud sync (Pro), these records and your yearly reading goal sync between your own devices signed in to the same Apple Account through your iCloud (Apple). The developer cannot see them. Cover images are not synced; each device fetches them again.

Book search and cover images

When you search for a book or fetch a cover, and only then, the app sends your search term (or the ISBN) to the public bibliographic database Open Library (Internet Archive). When you scan the barcode of a Japanese book (an ISBN starting with 978-4), the ISBN is sent to openBD, the bibliographic database of Japanese publishers. Only that search term is sent — never the contents of your shelf, your name, or any device identifier. You can also add a book by typing its title yourself, which sends nothing at all.

If Open Library returns few matches for a search — common for books in Chinese, Korean, Russian or Ukrainian — the same search term is also sent to Apple's iTunes Search API (Apple Books search) to find the book and its cover. Only the search term is sent. When you add a book found this way, its cover image from Apple Books is downloaded to your device.

Only if you turn on “Also find covers on Apple Books” in Settings, the title and author of books without a cover are sent to Apple's iTunes Search API (Apple Books search), and the cover image found there is downloaded. This setting is off by default and can be turned off at any time (turning it off deletes the covers that came from Apple Books from your device).

Importing a Goodreads export file (CSV, Pro) reads the file on your device only; its contents are never sent to the developer or any third party. To fetch covers for imported books, the ISBN is sent to Open Library as above (and, if the Apple Books setting is on, the title and author are sent to Apple).

Camera

The camera is used only to read the ISBN barcode on the back of a book. Recognition happens on the device; no image is stored or transmitted.

Anonymous usage

To improve the app, the following anonymous records are sent to the developer's server: the kind of action taken (such as the app being opened), which screen showed the Pro offer and what triggered it, that a widget was displayed and its size, the number of books added (never their titles), app and OS version, language setting, a random identifier generated on the device (removed when you delete the app), and the country of the connection (the IP address is not stored).

Never sent: the titles or authors of your books, your name, email, phone number or address, the advertising identifier (IDFA) or device identifier (IDFV), your location, your IP address, or any camera image.

These records are not used for advertising or ad measurement, are not sold to data brokers, and are not linked to any third-party tracking data. No third-party analytics SDK is embedded.

You can stop this at any time in the app: Settings (gear) → Privacy → “Share anonymous usage”. Turning it off also deletes anything not yet sent.

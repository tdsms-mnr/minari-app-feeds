Moviedeck Privacy Policy

Your data

Moviedeck ("the app") stores your answers about films, your watchlist, what you have watched and passed on, and your reactions only on your device. Recommendations are chosen on your device. The app does not send any of this to the developer's servers and does not share it with third parties. There is no account and no advertising.

Film list

Once a day the app downloads the current list of films and where they are streaming from the developer's server. The request carries nothing about you or your tastes.

Posters and streaming services

Posters are loaded directly from the image server of Movie of the Night, the provider of the streaming availability data. When you tap "Open in …", the app opens that streaming service's app or website; what happens there is covered by that service's own privacy policy.

Purchases

Moviedeck Pro is purchased through Apple. The developer does not receive your name, Apple ID or payment details.

Anonymous usage

To improve the app, the following anonymous records are sent to the developer's server.

  • The kind of action taken, such as opening the app or reaching the daily limit of films
  • Whether Pro is active
  • Which screen showed the Pro offer, and the result of a purchase (completed, cancelled, failed)
  • App version, OS version and language setting
  • A random identifier generated on the device (deleted when you delete the app)
  • The country of the connection (IP addresses are not stored)

The following are never sent:

  • The films you answered about, your watchlist or your reactions
  • Contact details such as name, email address, phone number or postal address
  • Advertising identifier (IDFA) or device identifier (IDFV)
  • Location
  • IP address

These records are not used to serve or measure advertising, are not sold or given to data brokers, and are not linked with any third party's tracking data. No third-party analytics SDK is included.

You can stop sending at any time from Settings (gear in Library) > "Share anonymous usage".

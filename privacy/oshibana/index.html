Oshibana Privacy Policy

日本語

If the English and Japanese versions differ, the Japanese text is authoritative.

Your data

Oshibana ("the app") stores your flowers — the photos, the cut-out flowers, the pressed specimens, names, places, dates — and your settings only on your device. The app does not send them to the developer's servers and does not share them with third parties. There is no account and no advertising.

Photos and cutouts

You can take a photo in the app or choose one with the iOS photo picker. The app receives only the photo you take or choose; it does not access your whole photo library. Cutting out the flower and making the pressed specimen are done on your device with Apple's Vision and Core Image frameworks. Photos never leave your device.

Place names

If a photo you choose already contains a location, the app sends only its latitude and longitude to Apple's geocoding service to get a place name for the label. The photo itself is not sent. The app does not ask for or use your current location; photos taken in the app have no location.

Face down

On iPhones that don't fold, the app reads the accelerometer to tell when the phone is face down. These readings are used only at that moment and are not stored or sent.

No usage data

The app has no analytics and sends no usage records to the developer. No third-party SDK is included.

Contact

When you write an email from "Contact us" in the app's Settings, the app version, iOS version, iPhone model and language are filled in (never your records or photos). You decide whether to send it and can delete them before sending.

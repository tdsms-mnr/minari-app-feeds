Hako Privacy Policy

日本語

If the English and Japanese versions differ, the Japanese text is authoritative.

Your data

Hako ("the app") stores your jewelry records — names, kinds, metals, stones, notes, dates, how each piece came to you, prices, the days you wore them, sets, care dates — together with the photos, certificate photos and PDFs you add, only on your device, in the app's own container (shared with the app's widget). The app does not send them to the developer's servers and does not share them with third parties. There is no account and no advertising.

Photos and cutouts

To add a piece, you can take a photo or choose one with the iOS photo picker. The app receives only the photo you take or choose; it does not access your whole photo library. Removing the background and splitting a photo of several pieces into separate pieces are done on your device with Apple's Vision framework. Photos never leave your device.

Certificates and warranties

Certificate photos and PDFs you attach are copied into the app's own container on your device. The original files are not changed and are never uploaded.

Loading a piece from a product link

When you enter a product link, the app loads that store's page and product image directly from the store's servers. Nothing passes through the developer's servers. As with ordinary web browsing, the store can see your IP address and similar connection details. The app never sends your records to the store. If you don't enter a link, this connection never happens.

Care reminders

When you set a next care date, the app schedules a notification on your device. Notifications are created by iOS on your device and are not sent through any server.

Anonymous usage

To improve the app, the following anonymous records are sent to the developer's server.

  • The kind of action taken, such as opening the app
  • Which screen showed the Pro offer, and which feature triggered it
  • The number of pieces (never their names, photos or prices)
  • App version, OS version and language setting
  • A random identifier generated on the device (deleted when you delete the app)
  • The country of the connection (IP addresses are not stored)

The following are never sent:

  • Your content — names, photos, certificates, prices, notes and wearing records
  • Contact details such as name, email address, phone number or postal address
  • Advertising identifier (IDFA) or device identifier (IDFV)
  • Location
  • IP address

These records are not used to serve or measure advertising, are not sold or given to data brokers, and are not linked with any third party's tracking data. No third-party analytics SDK is included.

You can stop sending at any time from Settings (gear) > Privacy > "Share anonymous usage".

Awayglass Privacy Policy

日本語

If the English and Japanese versions differ, the Japanese text is authoritative.

Your data

Awayglass ("the app") stores the times your phone was closed or face down, the size of your glass and your settings only on your device. The app does not send them to the developer's servers and does not share them with third parties. There is no account and no advertising.

Closing and face down

To know when the phone is closed, the app looks at the width of its own screen. On iPhones that don't fold, it reads the accelerometer to tell when the phone is face down. These readings are used only at that moment and are not stored or sent.

Lock Screen

The Lock Screen timer is a Live Activity created by iOS on your device. It does not go through any server.

Export

A CSV file is made only when you choose to export, and goes only where you send it.

In-app purchase

Awayglass Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your payment details. Whether you own Pro is checked on your device with Apple's StoreKit.

No usage data

The app has no analytics and sends no usage records to the developer. No third-party SDK is included.

Contact

When you write an email from "Contact us" in the app's Settings, the app version, iOS version, iPhone model and language are filled in (never your records or photos). You decide whether to send it and can delete them before sending.

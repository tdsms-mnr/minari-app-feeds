Backlabel Privacy Policy

日本語

If the English and Japanese versions differ, the Japanese text is authoritative.

Your data

Backlabel ("the app") stores the labels you save — the photo, the ingredients it read, your notes and your list of ingredients to avoid — and your settings only on your device. The app does not send them to the developer's servers and does not share them with third parties. There is no account and no advertising.

Camera and photos

The app uses the camera to take a photo of a label. When you choose a photo instead, you pick it in the system photo picker; the app receives only the photo you pick and does not read the rest of your photo library.

Reading and translating

Text on the label is read on your device with Apple's Vision framework. The directions and cautions are translated on your device with Apple's Translation framework; if a language is not on your device yet, iOS may download it from Apple. The developer receives nothing from this.

In-app purchase

Backlabel Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your payment details. Whether you own Pro is checked on your device with Apple's StoreKit.

No usage data

The app has no analytics and sends no usage records to the developer. No third-party SDK is included.

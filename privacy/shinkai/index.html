Shinkai Privacy Policy

日本語

If the English and Japanese versions differ, the Japanese text is authoritative.

Your data

Shinkai ("the app") stores your journal — which seas you started, your companion and the name you gave it, what you have observed, and the observation cards you take — only on your device, in the app's own container (shared with the app's widget). The app does not send them to the developer's servers and does not share them with third parties. There is no account and no advertising.

Saving to Photos

When you tap Save on an observation card, the app asks for permission to add photos and adds only that card to your photo library. It never reads your photo library.

Notifications

If you turn them on, the app schedules a notification on your device for the day a new resident or visitor arrives. Notifications are created by iOS on your device and are not sent through any server.

News from the developer

To show news, the app downloads a small file from the developer's server. Nothing about you is sent with this request beyond what any web request carries (such as your IP address), and the developer does not store it.

Anonymous usage

To learn which parts of the app people enjoy, the following anonymous records are sent to the developer's server.

  • The kind of action taken, such as opening the app, lighting a resident, meeting a visitor or taking a card
  • Which sea and which kind of animal it was, and the day number in that sea
  • App version, OS version and language setting
  • A random identifier generated on the device (deleted when you delete the app)
  • The country of the connection (IP addresses are not stored)

The following are never sent:

  • The name you gave your companion, and your observation cards
  • Contact details such as name, email address, phone number or postal address
  • Advertising identifier (IDFA) or device identifier (IDFV)
  • Location
  • IP address

These records are not used to serve or measure advertising, are not sold or given to data brokers, and are not linked with any third party's tracking data. No third-party analytics SDK is included.

You can stop sending at any time from Settings (gear) > Privacy > "Share anonymous usage".

Rowmark Privacy Policy

日本語

English version of the policy above. If the two differ, the Japanese text is authoritative.

Your data

Rowmark ("the app") stores your projects' names, row counts, targets and repeat settings only on your device, in the app's own container. The app does not send them to the developer's servers and does not share them with third parties. There is no account and no advertising.

From version 1.1, patterns you attach to a project (a PDF or photo you choose) are also stored only on your device, in the app's own container, and are never sent to the developer's servers. Deleting a project deletes its pattern too.

iCloud sync (version 1.3 and later, Pro)

Only if you have Pro and keep iCloud sync on, your projects (names, row counts, targets and repeats) are stored in your own iCloud (the iCloud key-value store provided by Apple) and synced only between your own iPhone and iPad signed in with the same Apple Account. Patterns (images and PDFs) stay on each device and are not synced. The developer cannot see this content, and nothing is sent to the developer’s servers. You can turn sync off in the app’s settings at any time; turning it off deletes nothing.

Anonymous usage

To improve the app, the following anonymous records are sent to the developer's server.

  • The kind of action taken, such as opening the app
  • Which screen showed the Pro offer, and which feature triggered it
  • That a widget was displayed, and its size
  • The number of projects (never their names or counts)
  • App version, OS version and language setting
  • A random identifier generated on the device (deleted when you delete the app)
  • The country of the connection (IP addresses are not stored)

The following are never sent:

  • Your content — project names and counts
  • Contact details such as name, email address, phone number or postal address
  • Advertising identifier (IDFA) or device identifier (IDFV)
  • Location
  • IP address

These records are not used to serve or measure advertising, are not sold or given to data brokers, and are not linked with any third party's tracking data. No third-party analytics SDK is included.

You can stop sending at any time from Settings (gear) > Privacy > "Share anonymous usage". Stopping also deletes any records not yet sent from the device.

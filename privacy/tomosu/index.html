Nightwick Privacy Policy

日本語

If the English and Japanese versions differ, the Japanese text is authoritative.

Your data

Nightwick ("the app") stores your candle records — names, photos, scents, weights, where and when you got them, and the nights you lit them — only on your device, in the app's own container (shared with the app's widget). The app does not send them to the developer's servers and does not share them with third parties. There is no account and no advertising.

iCloud (Nightwick Pro)

If you use Nightwick Pro, your records and photos are also kept in your own private iCloud database, so they stay when you change iPhones. This uses Apple's CloudKit and your Apple Account. The developer cannot see or access this data, and it never passes through the developer's servers. Without Pro, nothing is stored in iCloud.

Photos

To add a photo, you choose one with the iOS photo picker. The app receives only the photo you choose; it does not access your whole photo library.

Adding a candle from a shop link

When you enter a shop page link, the app loads that page and its product image directly from the shop's servers. Nothing passes through the developer's servers. As with ordinary web browsing, the shop can see your IP address and similar connection details. The app never sends your records to the shop. If you don't enter a link, this connection never happens.

Lock Screen and widget

The Lock Screen display while a candle burns and the Home Screen widget are drawn by iOS on your device from data the app keeps in its own container. They do not go through any server.

Anonymous usage

To improve the app, the following anonymous records are sent to the developer's server.

  • The kind of action taken, such as opening the app, adding a candle or lighting one
  • Which screen showed the Pro offer, and the result of a purchase (completed, cancelled, failed)
  • The number of candles (never their names, photos or scents)
  • The scent family suggested to you, as one of seven general families (never the scents you entered)
  • App version, OS version and language setting
  • A random identifier generated on the device (deleted when you delete the app)
  • The country of the connection (IP addresses are not stored)

The following are never sent:

  • Your content — names, photos, scents, links and the nights you lit them
  • Contact details such as name, email address, phone number or postal address
  • Advertising identifier (IDFA) or device identifier (IDFV)
  • Location
  • IP address

These records are not used to serve or measure advertising, are not sold or given to data brokers, and are not linked with any third party's tracking data. No third-party analytics SDK is included.

You can stop sending at any time from Settings (gear) > Privacy > "Share usage data".

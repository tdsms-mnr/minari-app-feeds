Departly Privacy Policy

Your data

Departly ("the app") stores your trip — your line, the stations you get on and off at, the minutes from your door to the platform, and the days you ride — only on your device, in the app's own container (shared with the app's widgets). The app does not send it to the developer's servers and does not share it with third parties. There is no account and no advertising.

Schedules

Train schedules are built into the app. Looking up your trains does not connect to any server.

Widgets and Live Activity

The Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets and the Live Activity are drawn by iOS on your device from data the app keeps in its own container. They do not go through any server.

Anonymous usage

To improve the app, the following anonymous records are sent to the developer's server.

  • The kind of action taken, such as opening the app, finishing or changing the setup, or starting a Live Activity
  • Whether a trip is set up and whether Pro is active
  • Which screen showed the Pro offer, and the result of a purchase (completed, cancelled, failed)
  • App version, OS version and language setting
  • A random identifier generated on the device (deleted when you delete the app)
  • The country of the connection (IP addresses are not stored)

The following are never sent:

  • Your trip — your line, stations, times and days
  • Contact details such as name, email address, phone number or postal address
  • Advertising identifier (IDFA) or device identifier (IDFV)
  • Location
  • IP address

These records are not used to serve or measure advertising, are not sold or given to data brokers, and are not linked with any third party's tracking data. No third-party analytics SDK is included.

You can stop sending at any time from Settings (gear) > "Share anonymous usage".
